Rock N Roller

Play Versions About

A freeware guitar-practice app that plays Rocksmith-format .psarc charts — built around freeware CDLC from the CustomsForge community. Runs entirely in your browser (Chromium recommended); a demo song is preloaded, and you can grant a local folder of your own CDLC.

▶ Play latest

Desktop builds for macOS and Windows are on the versions page and itch.io.

Not affiliated with Ubisoft or Rocksmith. No official DLC is hosted or distributed here. Your song files never leave your machine — the web app reads granted folders locally in the browser.
